# Bali Villa Console

Operations console, finance back office, HR and payroll, employee area and front-desk phone app for a villa operator with 20 units across 4 locations in Bali. Built in the WIT UI style, version 2: one Vite + React 19 + Tailwind 4 app in clean architecture, light theme by default with a dark theme from the same tokens.

## Modules

- Dashboard: executive (revenue, occupancy, ADR, cash, payroll), operational and financial views with period, location, villa, unit, source and custom date filters; cards or tables.
- Property: each property operates as a Hotel, a Villa, or Hotel and villas, and every rule keys on the unit kind (hotel room or villa): what is sold, the rate basis, minimum stay (1 night or 2), the deposit at check-in, stayover cleaning on a schedule, and Rooms and Villas split in revenue figures. Hotel rooms carry a floor and sell by type; a villa may rent whole as one group booking. Locations, villas with a profile (address and map pin, contact, check-in and check-out times, licence, bank, cancellation policy, deposit, house rules) and photos, room types, units with capacity (adults, children, extra beds), beds, size, view, photos and a status (active, cleaning, maintenance, inactive) that changes with a reason and expected return, a status history fed by people, housekeeping and maintenance, and a unit status board that flags overdue units. Facilities and amenities, a New property wizard that starts from the operation and sets up hotel rooms (with a room number generator) and villas each with their own defaults, unit blocks. Booking, room moves and check-in refuse a party the unit cannot sleep. A room type of identical rooms can sell by type: guests book the type and the operator assigns the room. Each room type carries a rate plan (weekday, weekend for Friday and Saturday nights, seasons at a fixed price or a surcharge) that prices direct stays on units without a live Airbnb listing. Stay restrictions per room type or unit (minimum and maximum stay, closed to arrival or departure, stop sell) refuse new direct stays unless a manager overrides with a reason, and a room type sold by type may overbook up to a set count, with walks to another unit or property recorded with their cost.
- Airbnb: host accounts, one listing per unit, manual Airbnb booking entry with the price Airbnb sold, fee, payout and payment status, account report. Each listing carries the one price list: a base price shared with the unit and per-night prices (weekends and the high season seeded higher) on a price calendar.
- Booking: direct and Airbnb bookings, live availability with interchangeable alternatives, calendar, overlap refusal, unit swap with history and actor, stay adjustment, lifecycle with no-show, notes, payment tracking, a guest folio per stay with every debit and credit on its transaction code (nights, discount, room service, posted charges, rebates, payments, refunds) that the check-out settles in one payment; a rebate corrects one posted line on its own code (past the discount limit share of the line it needs someone who may approve bookings), a delivery made by mistake is voided only on its business day, and a credit shows Refund due and goes back against the payment it came in on through approval, and group bookings of several units with all-or-nothing availability, one group deposit and per-stay or master billing. A direct stay sells at the Airbnb listing price per night, or at the room type's rate plan when the unit has no live listing; staff can override the rate or give a percent or rupiah discount with a reason, and going past the discount limit (10% by default, on the payment policy) needs someone who may approve bookings. Airbnb stays keep the price the channel sold, extra nights included. Stays booked against a room type show as Unassigned in the list, in their own calendar lane and in the bell until the front desk assigns a room; check-in needs one, and the type never sells past its rooms on any night. A night audit at `/night-audit` closes each business date, shown in the header apart from the calendar date: a checklist with links, no-shows for arrivals that never came, the night's room posted on every stay in house (nights ahead show as a forecast on the folio, estimated at check-out) and the date rolled forward once.
- Guest: profiles, stay history, check-in and check-out checklists with deposit and departure charges. Foreign guests carry passport, entry and visa data that check-in requires, with a daily foreign guest list and an export for the APOA immigration report. Profiles hold preferences, allergies, VIP level, tags and a blacklist, and duplicates merge. Guests get WhatsApp and email messages from templates (confirmation, payment, pre-arrival, welcome, checkout reminder, thank you with an e-invoice link), fill an online check-in at `/checkin/:token` that the desk verifies, and lost and found items are logged with a photo and tracked to return or disposal.
- Room services: a menu per villa (food, breakfast, laundry, transfers, spa, tours, extras) with hours, notice, photos and service partners with their cost; orders from the desk or from the guest through a QR card per room at `/order/:token`; an order board on the desk and the phone with late orders flagged; delivered orders on the guest's bill, check-out held until they are paid, payment by cash, transfer or a QRIS, VA or card link, income booked per category and partner bills as cash-outs, all through approval; items on the house with permission; four room service reports. Free requests (towels, cleaning, repairs) keep their own board.
- Maintenance: work orders apart from guest requests (parts, labour and vendor estimate, before and after photos, unit block while open, itemised expenses where each parts purchase, vendor invoice or labour payment is its own cash-out from the cashbox or the bank through the approval chain, with correction and resubmit after a rejection), preventive plans that open jobs when due, an asset register with warranty and repair cost, vendors with spend, a housekeeping board from dirty to ready with an inspection checklist, and five maintenance reports. Housekeeping assigns room tasks to attendants on shift by credits and a daily quota, attendants work their list on the phone, and linen is tracked per villa from rooms to soiled, laundry and back, with laundry batches and losses.
- Payments: deposit or full payment taken with a booking, cash and bank transfer recorded at the desk, QRIS, virtual account and card through a payment link with a guest checkout at `/pay/:token` (demo gateway buttons simulate success or failure), Airbnb payouts matched against the bank, refunds, receivables aging, printable invoices, gateway fees, and a payment policy. Every payment lands as a cash-in for approval. Changing the stay reprices it: a longer stay or a dearer unit raises the balance, a shorter one offers the overpayment back as a refund. Cancelling applies the policy (the deposit back when cancelled early enough, kept as room revenue otherwise) and a no-show keeps what was paid; both close open payment links. Direct prices and rate plans read net by default, so the quote, booking total, deposit, balance, payment links, checkout page and invoice carry 10% service and 10% PB1 on top ("Prices exclude 10% service and 10% PB1"), while Airbnb prices, menu prices and stays from before the console read inclusive. A city ledger at `/city-ledger` bills companies and travel agents after the stay: a direct booking or group carries the account and what it pays (all charges, room only, room and breakfast) within its credit limit, the desk transfers that part of the folio to the account at check-out (1200 to 1210), and finance issues CL-YYYY-MM-NNN invoices with the tax as billed and an agent's commission taken off (6750), emails them through the guest messaging outbox, spreads payments over them through the cash-in approval chain with any overpayment kept as credit, writes off small balances through a manual journal (6950), and prints a statement of account with aging.
- Receipts and evidence: photos and PDFs up to 5 MB, picked, dropped or photographed on the phone, on cash entries, work order expenses, desk payments, refunds and the guest checkout (transfer receipt). They travel to the ledger entry, approvers see them in the inbox, and they lock once the entry is posted. Cash-outs from a set amount (Rp 1.000.000 by default, on the Categories page) need one before approval.
- Record flow: booking, payment, cash entry, work order, payroll period and leave or overtime requests show where they sit in their flow, each step linking to the record it leads to.
- Finance: cashboxes and bank, cash in and out with reference and attachments, cash transfer between accounts, balance adjustment, cash count reconciliation, categories per villa, posting and reversal. Petty cash per villa with a float, a custodian, spends that always carry a receipt, top-ups back to the float through approval, counts, a low-balance alert, and a villa manager's view limited to their villa. Front desk cashier shifts open on a counted float, carry every desk payment and cash entry, and close on a counted drawer, with differences past the tolerance signed off and posted through the cash count.
- Accounting: a chart of accounts with each category and cash account mapped to a ledger account, room revenue (4100), room service revenue per department (4210 to 4270) and other income (4900), transaction codes on every folio line (ROOM, FNB, MINI, LDRY, TRNS, SPA, TOUR, XTRA, LATE, DMG and one per payment method) that decide the revenue account of guest money on the Transaction codes page while the Categories page maps the rest, a revenue by code and department report, a journal written on every posting, reversal, transfer, adjustment, cash count and payroll close, manual adjusting journals through approval (signed from the approval inbox and counted in the pending badge), general ledger, trial balance, income statement and balance sheet by villa, and month close. The books run on accruals: a delivered order books its room service revenue (net of items on the house and discounts) and a folio or departure charge its code's revenue on the stay's receivable the day it happens, a rebate takes it back off on the same account, each night audit bills that night's room on the stay's receivable at the night's rate less its discount share (an Airbnb stay at the channel price, its fee as a cost at check-out and the payout on the same receivable), room money sits on Guest deposits until the first audited night moves it over, check-out bills the nights no audit reached, and the payments pay that receivable down so a settled stay ends at zero; a delivered order puts its partner cost on Payables to service partners until the partner bill posts; and Pay statutory on a closed payroll submits the BPJS and PPh 21 remittances that pay those payables down, with receivable by stay and partner balance reports. Service charge and PB1 are set on the Tax and service page (rates by effective date, the codes that carry each, PB1 on the amount plus service by default): every taxable folio posting writes its SVC and PB1 lines, the books credit Service charge payable (2400), which the payroll service charge pool reads and payroll close pays down, and PB1 payable (2330), which Pay PB1 remits per regency for a closed month, with PB1 by regency and service charge by villa reports.
- Stock: items for the minibar, amenities and linen, a store per villa with on hand and average cost, purchases that become cash-outs for approval, an amenity kit per room type issued on each turnover, minibar checks that bill the guest through room service, transfers, write-offs, counts with variance, low stock alerts and four stock reports.
- Villa owners: owners and management contracts per villa (fee on room revenue, gross revenue or GOP, reserve, owner stays), monthly owner statements that finance closes, the Owner approves and the bank pays, booked through owner payables, where a month below zero carries forward to the next statement (or the owner tops it up) and finance can reopen an unpaid statement, and an owner portal at `/portal` where each investor sees only their villas, statements, payouts and stay requests.
- Approval: configurable rules by kind, villa and amount with multi-level chains (Villa manager, Finance, Owner), approve or reject with a note, resubmit, approval history, an inbox per role, separation between author and approver.
- HR: employees with villa allocation, shift roster, attendance, leave and overtime with approval, employee loans and salary advances, KPI reviews, announcements.
- Payroll: monthly periods calculated from salary, allowances, overtime, service charge, THR, bonus, reimbursement, late and absence deductions, loan instalments, BPJS and a simplified PPh 21; adjustments with reasons; approve, close (posts the bank entry and books loan repayments), reopen with a reason, publish payslips; configurable settings.
- Employee area: home with attendance, shifts, leave balance, latest payslip, KPI and announcements; leave, overtime and loan requests; payslips.
- Reports: management reports (cash, cash flow, income, expense, profit and loss by villa, location and portfolio, approvals, revenue by source, account and listing, discounts given by villa and by person, petty cash by villa, occupancy, bookings, unit movements, guest history, payroll summary, by villa and by location, attendance, leave, overtime) with date, scope and status filters and CSV export for Excel. A revenue dashboard at `/revenue` shows occupancy, ADR, RevPAR, booking pace and a month-end forecast, and `/revenue/budget` sets a budget per villa and month against the actual.
- Access and security: sign-in, users, roles with a permission matrix per module and action, villa and location scoping, audit trail with before and after values, posted entries never edited.
- Master data: one page per reference table, approval rules and shifts included.
- Guide at `/guide`: every module with its features, the roles allowed by the permission matrix and the rules it enforces, each flow as numbered steps with its system detail (use case, records and status changes, ledger entries, approval levels, audit, refusals, reports), a module connection map and the lifecycle of each main record.
- Public site at `/stay`: the Bali Villas company site with its own layout, type and palette, built from the console's villas, room types, prices, stay rules, free rooms and HR roles without sign-in, with an about page at `/stay/about`, a page per villa with a free-room check and quote, a search across villas, and a request to book or an owner's note that lands in Web enquiries at `/enquiries`, where reservations answers it and converts a stay into a prefilled booking.
- Front desk app at `/m`: today with the business date, free nights, room services, cashbox and villa petty cash, the night audit checklist read-only with a link to the console, my area.

## Run

```bash
pnpm install
pnpm dev
```

The app runs on in-memory sample data that it keeps in the browser for the day; reset it from Settings. Sign in with any of the demo logins listed on the sign-in page (password `villa123`): owner, villa manager, finance, HR, reservation, staff and administrator. `/login?as=wayan&next=/me` signs in as a seeded user directly. Set `VITE_API_URL` to the Go API base (for example `https://host/api/v1`) to run against the backend.

### Docker

`api/docker-compose.yml` runs the whole stack: Postgres, the Go API and the console. The console image builds the app with Vite and serves it from nginx, which proxies `/api/` to the API on the same origin, so the auth cookie needs no CORS.

```bash
cd api && docker compose up -d --build
```

Open http://localhost:8000. To change a port, set `WEB_PORT` (default 8000), `API_PORT` (8080) or `POSTGRES_PORT` (55432). Split mode puts the console in front of the gateway:

```bash
cd api && WEB_API_UPSTREAM=gateway:8090 docker compose --profile split up -d --build --scale monolith=0
```

Build with `WEB_API_URL=` (empty) for the mock-data demo with no API behind it.

## Checks

```bash
pnpm check
```

Runs the TypeScript build, oxlint and Vitest (domain rules, use cases over the mock adapters, seed integrity, the architecture rule).

## Layout

```
src/lib             pure helpers
src/domain          types, labels and rules, no React
src/application     ports, use cases and the report registry
src/infrastructure  mock adapters (seed data, rules, persistence, sessions, audit) and the HTTP adapter
src/presentation    UI kit, charts, hooks, layouts, feature pages
src/app             composition root and router
```
